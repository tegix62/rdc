01 Do this one
Highest leverage · one CSS variable

The type contradicts the pitch

The site says brand designer, illustrator and typographer. It's set in Inter Tight — the most neutral sans of the last decade. Every mark in your grid is drawn by hand; the frame around them is the Figma default.

Above — the claim, set in Inter Tight.

Below — the evidence, drawn by you. Heavy, condensed, high-contrast. None of it neutral.

The gap between those two plates is the whole argument. A heritage-apparel client lands on a page whose frame reads as SaaS, then scrolls into work that reads as a 1970s band tee.

The move: one display face, headings only. Body copy stays — Gothic A1 is doing its job and readability isn't the problem. And the elegant version is sitting in your own CMS already: set the site in a face you actually credit. Then the site isn't claiming the practice, it's an instance of it.

02 Most distinctive

The archive is a place, not a switch

You called print mode "an experimental tool on my side for the time being." It's the only thing on this site no competitor has, and it demonstrates the heritage-craft claim instead of asserting it. Right now it's a recessed button in a corner that most visitors will never press.

Screen — as it ships today

Bone stock, one ink — real output, not a mockup

An archive sheet — logomarks, no titles, no borders. The thing you described: strong enough to speak without labels.

The move: give it a name that isn't "print mode" — it's a riso zine treatment, and the name should be yours — then let one page live in it permanently instead of making it a toggle to discover. A page that is always one ink on stock is a position. A toggle is a party trick.

Built — the whole treatment, already Yours — the name, and whether it's permanent Mine — the page

The through-line

All four are the same move: the site currently describes a hand-made practice inside a machine-made frame. Typography is the cheapest and highest-leverage. The permanent archive is the most distinctive. The principal-type index is the most recognisably you.
