# Privacy policy — draft for your review

**This is a draft, not legal advice.** I'm not a lawyer. What I can vouch for is
the *factual* part: every third party named below was found by auditing the
actual code, not assumed. The full list of external hosts this site contacts is:

```
cdn.sanity.io          every image on the site
cdnjs.cloudflare.com   the grid layout script, /portfolio only
unpkg.com              the image-loading script, /portfolio only
i.ytimg.com            YouTube poster frames, on pages with a video
www.youtube.com        only after someone presses play
player.vimeo.com       only after someone presses play
tally.so               only when someone clicks Contact
```

And, as verified by grep across the whole source: **no analytics, no advertising
pixel, no Meta Pixel, no Google Tag Manager, no session recording, no
third-party fonts.** The Meta Pixel that runs on the Webflow site did not come
across in the port. If you decide to add it back, several statements below stop
being true and this needs rewriting — that's the single decision that changes
this document.

## Privacy Policy

I'm Chris Rumeau. I run Rumeau Design Co, a one-person design studio. This page
explains what happens to your information when you visit this site or get in
touch. It's short because the site does very little with your data.

### The short version

This site has no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies. I don't
know who visits it. If you contact me, I get your message and whatever contact
details you chose to include, and I use them to reply to you.

### What I collect when you just look at the site

Nothing that identifies you. There is no analytics software on this site, no
advertising or social media pixel, and no session recording. I have no idea how
many people visit, which pages they read, or where they came from.

### Cookies

This site sets no cookies.

If you switch the portfolio grid to Archive view, that preference is saved in
your browser's local storage so the page remembers it next time. It never
leaves your device and I never see it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

### Companies that help run the site

Loading a web page means your browser asks other computers for files, and those
computers can see your IP address as part of that request. For this site:

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site and serves every page.
- **Sanity** hosts the images and video files.
- **Cloudflare's cdnjs and unpkg** serve two small layout scripts, on the
  Portfolio page only.
- **YouTube** serves the still preview image on pages that have a video.

None of these are given your information by me. They see the request your
browser makes, which is how the web works. Each has its own privacy policy.

### Video

Videos on this site show a still image until you press play. Nothing is loaded
from YouTube or Vimeo, and neither company can set cookies or see you, unless
you actually choose to start a video. Once you do, you're interacting with them
under their own terms.

### When you contact me

The Contact buttons take you to a form hosted by **Tally**. When you submit it,
Tally passes me what you wrote — typically your name, your email address, and
your message.

I use that to reply to you and to discuss possible work. I don't add you to a
mailing list, I don't sell it, and I don't share it with anyone else unless
you've asked me to work with a collaborator on your project.

Project correspondence: kept while we're working together and for as long as I
need it afterwards for tax and business records.
